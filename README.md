A simple music player for gorilla tag.<br>
Can load in custom songs, shuffle, play, and swap speaker songs for other songs.<br>
(R) is for refreshing the song page.<br>
minus button next to songs selects the song.<br>
hashtag button stops and unshuffles the playlist.<br>
Songs are located in GS Files/Sounds.<br>
